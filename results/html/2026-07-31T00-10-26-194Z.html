This benchmark compared g-mesh to baseline grep/Read/Glob across 20 tasks. The results show marginal efficiency gains paired with stronger correctness.

The comparison does have reliability concerns worth noting. Additionally, one task declared to have parity between approaches (ex-find-callees-mutateelement) showed a surprisingly large 32% token difference, raising questions about task classification accuracy or hidden variance in the comparison.

For adoption: if your primary concern is correctness on complex navigation queries, g-mesh shows an advantage. If you're optimizing purely for token efficiency, the 2% improvement is too small to justify switching costs. If task classification reliability matters to you, these prediction mismatches suggest treating performance claims skeptically until validated on your own workloads.
